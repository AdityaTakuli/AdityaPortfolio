ArchJobs

Case study / Architecture job feed & tracker Node.js · Express · React · MySQL · Gmail API · LLMs

Every architecture, interiors and BIM internship and fresher job in India, on one page. ArchJobs pulls openings from Google Jobs, firms' own careers feeds and Gmail job alerts, merges the duplicates, labels each role with budgeted LLM calls, and keeps an application tracker that moves itself forward as replies arrive.

02

The pipeline

A job board is easy; a feed you can trust is not. The same role shows up on five sites, half the listings are for senior architects, and a third are already closed. ArchJobs runs every posting through one ingest path before anyone sees it.

  1. 01

    Collect

    Google Jobs searches twice a day, paced against the monthly SerpApi quota. Workday and SmartRecruiters careers feeds every morning. Gmail job alerts every 30 minutes.

  2. 02

    Merge

    Cities, company suffixes and title noise are normalised, then roles are deduped on company, title and city. Each keeps its best apply link: the firm's own site first, reposting sites last.

  3. 03

    Label

    An LLM returns strict JSON: relevant or not, internship or fresher, BIM, software, stipend, years of experience. If every model is down or out of budget, keyword rules stand in and an hourly retry sends it back.

  4. 04

    Retire

    A posting leaves every list the moment its apply-by date passes or its link returns a 404 or a "no longer accepting" page. Deleted roles are remembered for 180 days so they cannot come back.

03

Architecture

  • AI

    AI inside a budget

    Every model call is checked against four limits first: tokens per day, requests per day, tokens per run and tokens per minute. Totals live in an ai_usage table, so every task and restart shares one budget.

  • @

    A tracker that reads replies

    A separate mail model labels each reply as applied, interview, assessment, offer or rejected, and moves the matching tracker entry forward, never backwards, keeping the recruiter as the contact.

  • ID

    Shared feed, private trackers

    The job list, AI labels and search quota are shared by everyone who is invited. Trackers, firms, searches, settings and digests belong to one Google account each.

04

Engineering decisions

  • Q

    Quota is spread, not spent

    Each run asks SerpApi how many searches are left and spreads them over the rest of the month. Query and city pairs rotate, so adding a city shares the quota instead of multiplying it.

  • ?

    Searches that forgive typos

    "revti" becomes Revit, "arch" expands, and a city typed into the keyword box moves to "where". The same search within six hours reuses stored results for free.

  • ✓

    Forged email cannot add jobs

    A job alert or reply only counts when Gmail's own Authentication-Results header shows the sender passed DMARC, or DKIM or SPF for the From domain.

  • ⇢

    Link checks cannot reach inside

    Every URL the server opens goes through one safe fetch: http(s) on ports 80 and 443 only, never localhost, private networks or cloud metadata, checked again at connect time and on every redirect.

  • 🔒

    Read-only, and it stays that way

    Gmail access is gmail.readonly, refresh tokens are encrypted with AES-256-GCM, email bodies are never stored, and disconnecting revokes the grant at Google.
