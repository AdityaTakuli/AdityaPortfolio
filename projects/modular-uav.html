Modular Unmanned Aerial Vehicle with Independent Thrust Vectoring and Stabilization Control

Patent / Unmanned aerial vehicles Modular · Tilt · Level · Swap · Fly

A drone that keeps its body level while it moves. Instead of tipping the whole airframe to steer, each of its four ducted rotors tilts on its own hinge, so cameras, sensors and parcels ride flat. The wings come off, and one bay takes either a battery or a payload.

The idea

Most multirotors move by tipping their whole airframe: pitch to go forward, roll to go sideways. Whatever they carry tips with them, so cameras need heavy gimbals and parcels slide. This drone keeps its body still and moves the thrust instead.

  • 01

    Steer thrust, not the body

    The rotors do the leaning. The fuselage, and everything mounted to it, stays level through every manoeuvre.

  • 02

    One hinge per rotor

    Each rotor tilts on its own, so thrust can be pointed independently at every corner of the craft.

  • 03

    A body that holds level

    Onboard sensing and a learned stabiliser keep correcting, so the body stays flat in gusts and turns.

  • 04

    Wings that come off

    Two wing modules attach to the body and detach for transport, repair or a different mission.

  • 05

    One bay, many missions

    A vertical bay in the body takes either a battery or a payload module, swapped between flights.
