System for Orchestrated Control of Physical Infrastructure

Patent / Physical infrastructure automation Sense · Unify · Decide · Guard · Act

A system that lets a building look after itself. It listens to the spaces it runs in, forms one consistent picture of what is happening, decides what should change, and refuses to move a single piece of hardware until that decision has been checked.

The idea

Buildings are full of sensors and switches that rarely agree with each other. Readings arrive out of step, devices act on their own, and the result is a space that is automated but not coordinated. This invention puts one orchestrating loop between what a space senses and what it does.

  • 01

    Listen to the room

    Sensors spread through a space capture what is actually happening in it: temperature, presence, light, air and power draw.

  • 02

    One picture of the truth

    Scattered readings are reconciled into a single, consistent view of the infrastructure, so every decision starts from the same facts.

  • 03

    Orchestrate, don't react

    Decisions are made with the whole space in view rather than one device and one threshold at a time.

  • 04

    Check before moving

    Every proposed action is checked before it touches hardware. Only approved commands go through.

  • 05

    Close the loop physically

    The loop ends in the real world: fans, lights, locks, pumps and motors, driven safely and on purpose.
